# Last House Standing

An idle zombie-defense game about upgrading a house on a grassy field and holding back the horde.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish the repository with GitHub Pages.

- Your house automatically targets and shoots approaching zombies.
- Earn coins for every zombie defeated and spend them on house, walls, garden traps, passive supplies, and weapons. Unlock the minigun, piercing laser, explosive rockets, chain lightning, a slowing thorn field, a long-recharge black hole, and stunning smoke bombs.
- Waves get tougher over time. If the house falls, rebuild and keep your upgrades.
- The game saves locally and supply sheds keep earning a little while you are away.

No build tools or external dependencies are required.
