# Last House Standing

An idle zombie-defense game about upgrading a house on a grassy field and holding back the horde.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish the repository with GitHub Pages.

- Your house automatically targets and shoots approaching zombies.
- Earn coins for every zombie defeated and spend them on house, walls, garden traps, passive supplies, and a growing arsenal: miniguns, lasers, rockets, chain lightning, thorn fields, black holes, smoke bombs, sniper rifles, cryo blasts, flamethrowers, drones, mortars, railguns, and artillery.
- Survive changing day/night skies, stack kill streaks for bonus coins, and prepare for a boss every fifth wave. Runners and brutes join the horde as waves rise.
- Use the 1×/2× control to speed up the defense. If the house falls, rebuild and keep your upgrades.
- Turn on synthesized sound effects and an original looping ambient soundtrack with the SFX and MUSIC controls. Audio starts only after you enable it.
- The game saves locally and supply sheds keep earning a little while you are away.

No build tools, audio downloads, or external dependencies are required.
